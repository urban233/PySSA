Run a monomer protein structure prediction:

  1. Select one or more monomer sequences in the PySSA Objects Panel.
  2. Click on Prediction and Monomer to open the prediction dialog.
  3. Verify the pre-filled sequence list in the dialog.
  4. Click on Start to begin the calculation.
  5. Wait for the job to finish. Progress is shown in the toolbar on Running Jobs.

⚠ CAUTION:

An internet connection is required for prediction.
