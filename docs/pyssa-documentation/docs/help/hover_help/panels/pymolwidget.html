PyMOL Viewer:

This is the interactive 3D molecular viewer powered by PyMOL. It displays protein structures of a protein or protein pair in 3D.

Navigation in the viewer:

  • Left-click and drag down to zoom in; drag up to zoom out.
  • Right-click and drag to rotate the protein or protein pair.
  • Hold the middle mouse button and drag to move the protein or protein pair.
  • Scroll the mouse wheel up to reveal more of the structure (reduce clipping); scroll down to hide more.

Loading structures:

  1. Select a protein or protein pair in the PySSA Objects Panel.
  2. Click Open Session in the toolbar, or right-click and choose Open Session from the context menu.
